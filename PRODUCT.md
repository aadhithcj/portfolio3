## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims**: Every capability claim must be backed by verifiable code, live demos, or documented engineering decisions.
2. **Tactile Craftsmanship**: Champion the neo-brutalist visual world with crisp edges, zero-blur offset shadows, and responsive mechanical feel.
3. **Scannable Depth**: Allow recruiters to grasp core skills and project impacts within seconds, while giving deep-divers architectural detail and commit history.
4. **Performance & Standards**: Zero unnecessary bloat, smooth 60fps animations, accessible color contrast (>= 7:1 for body copy), and fluid mobile responsiveness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adhere to WCAG AA/AAA contrast ratios across both light and dark themes.
- Enforce visible, high-contrast keyboard focus indicators (`focus-visible:ring-2`).
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by reducing or disabling non-essential decorative animations.
- Ensure assistive screen-reader access with descriptive aria labels on interactive stickers, planet widgets, and icon-only links.
